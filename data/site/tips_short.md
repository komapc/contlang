- Quote only: proper names, exact numbers (`"3"`), Latin names of species (`"Canis"`), chemical names, institutions/titles/technical terms that have no root (`"Parliament"`). Never quote ordinary words.
- Actions without a root: `DO | i` (do, cause) or `SAY | i`. Difficult = `DO CAN(=-3) | a`. "Maybe" is the form label `M-3`, not a root. "And" is the particle `AND`, never JOIN/SAME/CHANGE.
- Amount: `MANY` (0 none ... +5 all). Food/drink: `CONSUME` (eat = `CONSUME(=+5) GRAIN(=+3) | i`, drink = `GRAIN(=-3)`).
- Degree is the form label I on the head word (very good = `GOOD(=+3) | a I+4`), never `BIG(=+5)` as "very". Small = `BIG(=-3)`. Earlier/later/before/after = label R, not TIME. `TIME(=+1)` reads as "soon".
- PART ladder: -5 crumb, -2 part/member, +2 whole/entire, +5 group. Society = `SOMEONE PART(=+5)`, member = `SOMEONE PART(=-2)`, herd = `THING(=0) PART(=+5)`.
- GRAIN (how stuff holds together, also solid/liquid/gas): -5 gas, steam, smoke, -3 liquid, flowing (water, honey), 0 loose/granular (sand, flour, dust, rice, gravel: add THING/PART/CONSUME to tell them apart), +5 one solid piece. Solid/whole/seamless = `GRAIN(=+5) | a`. Do not write "seamless" with `JOIN(=-5)` (reads as "separate"). Continuous in time: `GRAIN(=+5) TIME | a A0` or `BEGIN(=0) PART(=+2) | a` (BEGIN(=0) = going on).
- Numbers and maths: number `MEASURE(=-5) MANY | o` or `MANY ABSTRACT(=+5) | o`; zero `MANY(=0) MEASURE(=-5) | o`; count (verb) `MEASURE MANY DO | i`; sum `MANY JOIN(=+4) MEASURE(=-5) | o`; equal `SAME(=+5) MEASURE | a`; calculate `ART(=-5) THINK MEASURE | i`; fraction `PART(=-2) MEASURE(=+5) | o`; percentage/ratio `MEASURE(=+5) ...`; mathematics `ART(=-5) MANY SEE | o`.
- ART axis: -5 calculation/technique ... +5 creative/humanities (poetry `ART(=+5) SAY | o`, physics `ART(=-4) GRAIN MOVE | o`). Person of a craft: `SOMEONE ART(=-4) DO | o` (engineer).
- Emotions: `FEEL` + `GOOD` + root: fear = `FEEL(=+4) GOOD(=-4) TIME(=+3) | i`; hope = `WANT(=+4) GOOD(=+4) TIME(=+3) | a`.
- Other axes: `VALUE` (-5 cheap ... +5 expensive), `TONE` (-5 rude ... +5 polite), `CARE` (-5 careless ... +5 careful; carefully = `CARE(=+3) | e`), `ABSTRACT` (-5 physical ... +5 mental; idea = `THINK ABSTRACT(=+5) | o`), `RULE` (-5 private ... +5 official; authority = `RULE(=+4) | o`), `CHANGE` (remain = `CHANGE(=-4) | i`, grow = `CHANGE BIG(=+3) | i`).
- Metaphors fail: use ABOVE/INSIDE/BIG only for real up/inside/size. Write values as `(=+3)`, `(=-2)`, `(=0)`, never `+0`.
- Written language is the root `TEXT` (`SAY` is spoken): write `TEXT | i`, read `CONSUME(=+5) TEXT | i`, writer `SOMEONE TEXT | o`, book `TEXT PART(=+2) | o`, letter `TEXT GIVE(=+3) SOMEONE | o`, library `PLACE TEXT PART(=+5) | o`.
- FIGHT axis: -5 game/sport ... 0 brawl/quarrel ... +5 war. game `FIGHT(=-5) | o`, player `SOMEONE FIGHT(=-5) | o`, sport `FIGHT(=-5) BODY | o`, war `FIGHT(=+5) | o`, weapon `THING(=-5) FIGHT(=+5) | o`, soldier `SOMEONE FIGHT(=+5) RULE(=+4) | o`.
- Tools: the roots describe the action and `DO(=+5)` (DO axis: -5 doer ... +5 tool) makes it the tool: knife `JOIN(=-4) TOUCH(=+4) DO(=+5) | o`, hammer `TOUCH(=+5) MOVE(=+4) DO(=+5) | o`, pen `TEXT DO(=+5) | o`, thermometer `MEASURE HEAT DO(=+5) | o`. Never `THING(=-5)` for tools (reads as stone).
- Special / general is the SAME axis: special, unique `SAME(=-4) | a`, especially `SAME(=-4) | e I+4`, ordinary `SAME(=+3) | a`, general `SAME(=+4) MANY(=+5) | a`.
