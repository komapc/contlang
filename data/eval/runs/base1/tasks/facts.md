## Task

`paras.md` has short English paragraphs, each under a header `## <id>`. For each paragraph write the 4 to 6 most important
atomic facts it states: one short, self-contained English statement per fact (who does what, to whom, where, when, why, what
something is like). Cover the main point first; skip style and minor wording. Each fact must be checkable on its own.
Write `facts.md` in the same layout: the `## <id>` header, then numbered facts `1. …`. No other text.
