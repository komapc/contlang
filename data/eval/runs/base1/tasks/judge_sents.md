original. Score how much of the ORIGINAL's meaning the READING conveys:
3 — the same meaning (wording may differ; small nuances may be lost);
2 — the main meaning is there (who does what, the main point), but some details are lost or wrong;
1 — related: the topic or a part of the meaning is there, but the main point is lost or wrong;
0 — off: unrelated or contradicting.
Judge meaning only, not style or fluency. Reply with one line per item, exactly "N. score", nothing else.
