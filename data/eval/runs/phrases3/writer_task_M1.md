# Encoder instructions (expressions)

You will encode English expressions of one or two words into min-co, one independent code per expression.
Encode the most common meaning of the whole expression (for a phrasal verb or an idiom — its idiomatic meaning).
A different person will decode your codes WITHOUT seeing the English, using only the spec below. Write each code so that
the reader recovers the meaning of the expression as surely as possible.

- Use ONLY the 45 roots below; never invent roots or use English words as roots. At most 3 roots per min-co word.
- Do not quote common words.
- Write every expression as ONE min-co word (one `ROOTS | FORM` with its marks), never two words, never a link.
