## Task

Each line is a min-co code for an English adjective, alone or with a word of degree. For each line give your best English
reading as a short expression (e.g. "slightly warm", "big", "extremely good") and the degree of the quality on this scale:
1 — barely, hardly; 2 — slightly, a little; 3 — plain, no degree word; 4 — very; 5 — extremely, utterly.

Reply in exactly this format, one line per code, nothing else:
1. reading | degree
2. reading | degree
