## Glosses

In these codes every word in braces is followed by a gloss in angle brackets: `{ PLACE LIVE | o D+3 }<house>`. The gloss is
the meaning the writer intended for that word, in base form. The marks after `|` (tense, number, negation, definiteness,
etc.) and the particles (E, PI, LA, AND, …) still apply as described above.
