# Task

Each line describes ONE common English noun by two other words: `X LINK Y`.
X is the kind of thing; Y narrows it. LINK:
- FROM: X is made of Y.
- FOR: X is made for, used for, or exists for Y.
- LIKE: X looks like or behaves like Y.
- PI: X is part of Y, is found in Y, or belongs to Y.

Give your best English word and 2 alternatives.
