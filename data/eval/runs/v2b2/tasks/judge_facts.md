## Task

`items.md` has numbered blocks. Each block has a READING (an English paragraph reconstructed by someone who did not see the
original) and a numbered list of FACTS from the original paragraph. For each fact, judge whether the READING states it:
- **2** — stated (wording may differ);
- **1** — partly: the gist is there but an important part is missing or wrong;
- **0** — not stated, or contradicted.
Judge each fact only by the READING; do not guess what the original said.
Write `scores.md` in the same layout: the block header `## N`, then one line per fact `k. score`. No other text.
