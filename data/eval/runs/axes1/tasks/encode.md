## Task

Two files:
1. `words.md` — numbered English words (noun, verb or adjective) with their intended sense. For each, write ONE code (one
   word or a phrase, as the spec allows) that lets a reader who knows only the spec guess the word (or a word of the same
   meaning) most surely. Nothing is quoted in this part. Write `words_out.md`: `N. code`, one line per item.
2. `sents.md` — numbered English sentences. Translate each sentence, in ONE pass, as one line. Write `sents_out.md`:
   `N. CODE`, one line per sentence.
No other text in either file.
