Phrases: the head word comes first, modifiers follow ("tomo moku" = restaurant, "jan pona" = friend, "ilo toki" = phone). "pi" groups several modifiers ("tomo pi telo nasa" = bar). A part of speech is not marked; the same phrase can be a noun, verb or adjective by position.
