For each item, list EVERY kind of error the READING makes against the ORIGINAL, using only these codes:
- W: a content word wrong (a noun, verb, adjective or adverb replaced by an unrelated or clearly different one)
- R: roles wrong — who does what to whom is swapped or misassigned (subject/object, doer/receiver)
- P: a person or thing referred to wrongly (he/she/they/it/I/you confused, or a pronoun turned into "someone/others", or the wrong referent)
- O: owner lost or wrong (his/their/of X)
- L: a relation of place, time, cause, purpose, instrument or accompaniment lost or wrong (in, to, from, with, because, for, by, before ...)
- T: tense, aspect or modality wrong (past/future, can/must/may, completed/ongoing)
- N: negation or quantity wrong (not, no, all, some, many, few, numbers)
- C: clause structure wrong (what is subordinate to what, condition/contrast/comparison lost, two ideas wrongly merged or split)
- V: voice wrong (passive vs active) 
- X: something added that is not in the original
Write "-" if the READING has no error. For each code also give the main one first.
Reply with one line per item, exactly "N. MAIN | other codes", e.g. "7. W | R L", "8. - |", nothing else.
