## Dictionary hints

Some words in these codes are common words of a shared dictionary: such a word is followed by its dictionary meaning in
angle brackets, `{ PLACE LIVE | o D+3 }<house>`, in base form. Words without `<…>` are not in the dictionary: read them from
their roots as described above. The marks after `|` (tense, number, negation, definiteness, etc.) and the particles
(E, PI, LA, AND, …) apply to every word as described above.
