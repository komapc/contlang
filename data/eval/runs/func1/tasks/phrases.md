## Phrases

A thing may be named by a phrase of up to 3 nouns: `X | o LINK Y | o`, or `X | o LINK Y | o LINK Z | o`.
X is the kind, and each next noun narrows the one before it. Each word has its own limit of 3 roots.
LINK is the first of these that fits:
- `FROM`: X is made of Y.
- `FOR`: X is made for Y, used to do Y.
- `AGAINST`: X protects from Y, stops or fights Y (`{ THING | o tool } AGAINST { GRAIN(=-3) ABOVE | o }` = umbrella:
  a tool against liquid from above).
- `LIKE`: X looks like Y.
- `PI`: X is part of Y, or is found in Y.
