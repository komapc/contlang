# min-co axis card

Each code is a min-co ROOT with a value on its axis: `ROOT(=v)`, v from -5 to +5.
Only the two ends of each axis are given. 0 is the middle of the axis; values between are positions between the ends
(±2 a little toward that end, ±4 close to that end).

- ABOVE: -5 below, down … +5 above, up
- ART: -5 technology, exact science … +5 arts, humanities
- BEGIN: -5 start … +5 end
- BIG: -5 tiny … +5 huge
- CAN: -5 impossible … +5 easy
- CARE: -5 careless, a mistake, fault … +5 careful, thorough, diligent
- CHANGE: -5 stay the same … +5 transform
- CONSUME: -5 give out … +5 take in
- FEEL: -5 calm … +5 excited, intense
- FIGHT: -5 game, sport … +5 war
- FRIEND: -5 enemy, hostile, cruel … +5 friend, friendly, kind
- GIVE: -5 grab, seize, steal … +5 give, donate, grant
- GOOD: -5 terrible … +5 excellent
- GRAIN: -5 gas … +5 one solid piece
- HEAT: -5 ice cold … +5 fire hot
- INSIDE: -5 outside … +5 inside
- JOIN: -5 separate, remove … +5 join, add, attach
- KNOW: -5 stupid, foolish, absurd … +5 know, inform, teach
- LAND: -5 sea, water … +5 dry land, ground
- LIVE: -5 dead … +5 alive
- LONG: -5 thread, cloth, textile … +5 beam, plank, wooden furniture
- MANY: -5 none, never … +5 all, always
- MEASURE: -5 an amount … +5 a ratio, rate, percentage, statistics
- MOOD: -5 tragic … +5 funny
- MOVE: -5 stand still … +5 rush
- NEAR: -5 far, abroad, journey … +5 here, nearby, downtown, center
- PART: -5 particle, crumb … +5 a group of many
- RULE: -5 private, informal … +5 law, official
- SAME: -5 opposite, different … +5 identical
- SAY: -5 whisper … +5 shout
- SIDE: -5 back, behind, backward; also going back, return … +5 front, ahead, forward; also coming up, ready
- SOMEONE: -5 they, others … +5 I
- THING: -5 stone, inanimate … +4 animal
- THINK: -5 doubt … +5 decide
- TIME: -5 long ago … +5 far future
- TOUCH: -5 soft, smooth, tender … +5 hard, stiff, tough, harsh
- VALUE: -5 cheap, a bargain, buying and selling … +5 precious, important, vital
- WANT: -5 hate, refuse … +5 love, crave
