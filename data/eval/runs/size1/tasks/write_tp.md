
## Task

`list.md` gives numbered English words (noun, verb or adjective) with their intended sense. For each, write THREE Toki Pona
phrases that let a reader who knows only the dictionary above guess the English word (or a word of the same meaning) most
surely:
1. exactly ONE Toki Pona word;
2. at most 2 words (one word again if that is clearest);
3. at most 3 words (one or two words again if that is clearest).
Every Toki Pona word counts, `pi` included. Each phrase is the best you can do under its limit.

Write `answers.md` with exactly one line per item, same numbering, the three phrases separated by ` || `:
`N. phrase1 || phrase2 || phrase3`. No other text.
