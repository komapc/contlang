How to read min-co

For those who know Toki Pona and Esperanto.

A min-co word is a short description, like jan pona. To read it is to find a semantic field: house, dwelling, hut are all correct answers to PLACE LIVE | o.

The word

JOIN(=-4) TOUCH(=+4) | o tool        separate + sharp, a tool → knife
  • Before the | is the meaning. The first root is the genus (jan in jan pona); each following one narrows it. One to three roots. The head comes first, as in Toki Pona (not vapor·ŝipo but “ship · steam”).
  • After the | is the form.
  • Part of speech: o i a e are Esperanto's -o -i -a -e.
  • The noun's role is an Esperanto suffix: agent -ist- (who does it, or a person of this field), tool -il-, result -aĵ-, quality -ec-.
  • Grammatical labels (see “The sentence”).

The root's scale

Most roots have an axis from −5 to +5. It is Esperanto's mal- stretched into a scale.

notation meaning
GOOD good/bad matters, the degree is not named
GOOD(=+5) / GOOD(=-5) a pole: bona / malbona
GOOD(=+3) / GOOD(=-3) closer to the pole
THING(=0) the middle of the axis: between stone and animal is a plant
INSIDE(=0) between “outside” and “inside”: boundary, edge, surface

Examples

THING(=+4) BIG(=+5) | o N+3          animal + huge, many           → elephants
BODY GOOD(=-5) HEAT(=+5) | o         body + very bad + heat        → fever
SAY ART | o agent                    speech + art, who does it     → poet
SOMEONE FRIEND(=+5) | o              someone + friendly            → friend
LAND(=0) GRAIN(=0) | o               shore + granular              → beach
LAND RULE LONG(=-5) | o              land under rule + line        → border

Two words

X | o LINK Y | o: X is the genus, Y narrows it. This is Toki Pona's pi, only made more precise by the link word:

  • FROM: X is made of Y;
  • FOR: for Y;
  • LIKE: resembles Y;
  • PI: a part of Y, or located in Y.
DO | o tool FOR THING(=-5) BIG(=+5) MOVE(=+3) | o      a tool for a big moving thing → steering wheel

The sentence

The order is as in Toki Pona: subject, predicate, E object. The predicate is recognized by the form | i, so LI is not needed (removed). An attribute or a noun as predicate is the same root with | i: PLACE INSIDE(=+5) LIVE | o BIG | i is “the house is big” (like tomo li suli). A condition is introduced with LA. AND means “and”, PE means “by whom” with the passive voice.

Grammar is labels on the head word, after the |: - T-2 / T+2: -is / -os; - M-5: ne; - ?: ĉu; - D+3: la; - N+3: plural; - C+3: “more”.

SOMEONE(=+5) | o CONSUME(=+5) | i T-2 E GRAIN(=+5) CONSUME(=+5) | o
I ate solid food → “I ate a pie”

Names and numbers are written in quotes: "Rome", "17".
