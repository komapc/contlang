Rethinking the language: where we are and what to change (2026-10)

Update (2026-10-14): the “close” measure

The main measure is now different: the share of a blind reader's first answers that a blind judge (Haiku) considers “close”, the same semantic field (2 close, 1 near, 0 miss). The rank among 10,721 words below is the earlier measure, kept for the record.

  • 1000 words (big1000): the site encoder 24%, Haiku with ENC_SPEC 52%.
  • Accepted (user's decision of 2026-10-14, without a blind test): the site encoder always encodes the base form and appends labels (discussed → the code of discuss + T-2, -ing → A0, an attributive participle → | a V-4, plural → N+3, degrees → C+3/C+5); bases outside the dictionary are taken from Numberbatch; for a homonym, one sense is encoded (WordNet; at almost equal frequency, the one nearest the word's vector). scripts/site/build_words.py → infl, extra, sense. Corrections of 2026-10-14: the part of speech of a word form follows Brown if the form occurs there ≥ 5 times and Brown sees a noun or an attribute (attacks, plans, projects, tears → noun, surprising → attribute; 157 forms); sense anchors without hypernyms (reverse reading: word first 29.9 → 32.1%).
  • Accepted: the axes of 8 roots (LONG, KNOW, MEASURE, TOUCH, NEAR, GIVE, VALUE, CARE) from the words around the learned atoms, and the role agent for people (fix1).
  • Blind tests of the encoder (not accepted): choosing the code by the reader model (reader2, +4.7% [−4.0 … +13.3]), an economical encoder with fewer roots and no zeros (occam, −1.3% [−8.0 … +5.3]).
  • Not accepted: separate codes for the senses of polysemous words (senses, −2%), the root KIND (kind, +5.6% at the threshold; as a head only +6.7% [−2.7 … +16], side_kind; user's decision of 2026-10-14), the roles tool and quality by WordNet.
  • Postponed: removing the PI pair from the site encoder.

The measure and the benchmark (earlier)

Reading accuracy is measured by the rank of the target word among the 10,721 words of the site dictionary: the words are ordered by cos with a blind reader's answer, and 1 means the answer is closest to the target. The geometric mean of ranks is taken over 200 readings: 100 dev words, two Haiku readers each. Details: data/eval/runs/toki.

A measure for “10 times more accurate”. Rank with synonyms: any word from the WordNet synsets of the target word in its part of speech counts as a hit (car instead of automobile is rank 1). The geometric mean of this rank is how many equally likely candidates the reader has left; log2 of the rank is the remaining uncertainty in bits. “10 times more accurate” = 10 times fewer candidates = 3.3 bits less.

  • For now min-co is 2.5 times more accurate than Toki Pona (by 1.2 bits). The 10× goal is a rank with synonyms of about 6.
  • Why not other measures:
  • 1 − cos is compressed from above and below: a 10 times smaller error is cos 0.93, almost exact words only; the difference between Toki Pona and min-co on it is 0.68 against 0.58.
  • the share of misses (outside the top 10 with synonyms): 142 against 121 of 200 hardly distinguishes the languages, and 10× (14 misses of 200) would mean almost error-free reading.
  • rank without synonyms penalizes car instead of automobile: synonyms share one rank, and the reader is not at fault.

Proposals

# proposal basis what it removes status
1 A short specification v2 (spec_v2.md, 4.8 KB instead of 11 KB) Blind test on the same 100 words: v2 / v1 = 1.19 (95%: 0.76–1.87), no difference The fourth root ABSTRACT, the exception for cardinal directions, BIG as “degree”, the old -o notation, three different descriptions of labels. One rule for axes: −5 the opposite pole, 0 the middle, +5 the full pole accepted (2026-10)
2 Roles in the form instead of the DO and HAPPEN axes: agent, tool, result, quality after | o (like -ist, -il, -aĵ, -ec in Esperanto) Part of v2. The coder used them in 10 words of 100: telegraph 18 → 2, but beef 1 → 114 (result out of place) Two axes that are not scales (actor … action … tool; cause … consequence) accepted with v2; since 2026-10 the site encoder gives agent to people (noun.person in WordNet): +19% “close” [+10 … +30], fix1
3 A list of frequent words with fixed codes (about 1000, from verified recipes). Composition for the rest The best of three answers is already at rank 21: the needed word is usually among the options but does not come first. A fixed word is read exactly (the site's recipes) — the only lever toward 10× that I see; it changes the principle “there is no fixed vocabulary”; not checked on these 100 words (how many of them would make the list was not counted)
4 Measure on sentences, not words toki_sents: the same 100 words in sentences. Toki Pona 62 → 26, min-co 28 → 40. Toki Pona / min-co = 0.65 (0.36–1.17), no difference. Context helps polysemous Toki Pona. min-co in a sentence encodes the word more coarsely, and the reader does not complete the code from the meaning — done: min-co's advantage on words disappeared in text
5 Site encoder: choose the roots that models use (a prior over the corpus of model codes, kNN), not only y ≈ x Math 137 against 51 for the model; fitting the dictionary did not move ② (anchor) — checked: moving model codes into the dictionary (distill) reads almost as before, not accepted
6 CARE is the attitude to work, TONE replaced by FRIEND (enemy … friend) For math the roots moved apart: cos of the centers 0.65 → 0.12 (learned dictionary 0.07). Blind test on 20 words (friend): FRIEND / TONE 1.45 (0.71–3.01), no difference; friend better, polite worse Two roots with close centers accepted (2026-10)
7 Do not remove rare roots: NEAR, WANT, BEGIN, SEX, FEEL, CHANGE, MEASURE (4–8 times in 458 codes) Each carries its own words (king, improve, unit…) — keep
8 The labels I, C, N hardly occur on words (2, 1, 1 times) They cannot be tested on words — decide after the test on sentences
9 One quotes rule: proper names, digits, scientific names of species and substances Part of v2 The category lists in encoding.md accepted with v2
10 A verb with a head root with a value = transition into that state (BIG(=+3) | i grow, LIVE(=-5) | i K+3 kill), without CHANGE verbs: 40 verbs, B / A = 0.89 (0.67–1.19), no difference. Better learn, forget, worse weaken, accelerate CHANGE in verbs of change of state not accepted: no difference
11 New roots from the worst words: light (dark … bright), strength (weak … strong), humidity (dry … wet), openness, fullness; clothing, help, electricity, word/name The verbs darken, brighten, weaken, strengthen, dry, open, close, fill, empty are bad in both variants (rank 50–1800): the meaning has no axis. Words from the Toki Pona test suit, assistant, current, note, sentence — candidates: need clusters and a blind test

encoding.md was rewritten in the v2 notation: roles instead of DO(=+5) and HAPPEN(=±n), without ABSTRACT and four-root cardinal directions. Recipes converted to roles were not checked separately.
