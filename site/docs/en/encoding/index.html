How to encode: techniques

The techniques were selected through blind tests (the coder sees only the specification). Each has a link to its test; no test means the hint has not been checked. Roots and axes are in lexicon.md, labels and particles in syntax.md, the model in model.md. Used by the mincode skill (.claude/skills/mincode/SKILL.md).

v2 (2026-10). The limit is three roots without exceptions; DO and HAPPEN have no axis, and their earlier values were replaced by form roles (| o agent, tool, result, quality); there is no root ABSTRACT; TONE was replaced by FRIEND (enemy … friend). The linked tests checked the earlier notation; recipes rewritten into roles were not checked in the new notation, except by the general v2 test (data/eval/runs/toki).

General

  • Actions without their own root go through DO | i E … (to do, to cause) or SAY | i … (to say, to ask) (roundtrip_30).
  • “Can, difficult, possible” is CAN (DO CAN(=-3) | a is difficult); “possibly, maybe” is the label M-3; there is no root MAYBE.
  • Quotes have one rule: proper names, digits and scientific names of species and substances: "Rome", "17", "Canis" dog, "Quercus" oak, "iodine" iodine. Everything else, including categories (animal, plant, bird are THING(=+4), THING(=0)…), positions and institutions, is assembled from roots. Earlier, terms (parliament, railway, furnace) were also put in quotes: in a test on 60 sentences (roundtrip_wiki_new4) this gave 2.35 of 3 against 1.85; v2 gave it up for the sake of one rule, and on words there was no difference.
  • Quantity is MANY (−5 none, −4 shortage, −3 almost none, −1 few, 0 some amount, +3 many, +5 all; the scale has been two-sided since 2026-10, data/eval/runs/many), exact numbers in digits in quotes.
  • ABOVE, INSIDE, BIG only for real “top / inside / size”; a metaphor (roof, coffee, desk) and an intensifier (BIG(=+5) as “very”) are almost always misread (roundtrip_200).
  • Do not overuse ABOVE for “important”: elect through ABOVE(=+4) reads as worship.
  • Food and drink are CONSUME, a container is THING INSIDE | o or PLACE INSIDE | o (the root CONTAINER was removed); the roots HEAR, KIND, WORD, PEOPLE no longer exist (PEOPLE = SOMEONE MANY(=+4) | o).

Connectors: and, also, therefore

  • “And, also, too” is the particle AND between the coordinated items: X AND Y, [ … ] AND [ … ], in a list before the last element; do not use JOIN, CHANGE, SAME as connectors (decoders read them literally: joined, changed; roundtrip_wiki4). Checked (roundtrip_wiki_new4): AND in 39 codes of 60, decoders read it as and / also (54 times), literal “joined” 2 instead of 8.
  • AND with modifiers (blind test, 2 coders × 2 decoders, 11 sentences; roundtrip_and_mod): the axes are taken by the particle itself, the word order is the original one. AND SAME(=-5) is but, while, whereas (4/4 correct); AND SAME(=-2) is although (4/4); AND HAPPEN(=+4) is therefore, so (2/2); AND HAPPEN(=-4) is because (2/2); AND BIG(=+4) is all the more, let alone (let alone 2/2, what is more reads as a plain and + emphasis); AND MAYBE(=-3) (now AND | M-3, not checked) is or (2/2). LA stays for if / when.

Against, cause, decision

  • “Against” (protest, refuse) goes through an explicit bearer: SOMEONE SAY WANT(=-5) | o (the test had four roots, SOMEONE MANY(=+4) SAY WANT(=-5), over the limit; the three-root variant was not checked); a bare WANT(=-5) is often ignored by the decoder.
  • Cause and consequence as objects are roles: HAPPEN | o agent cause, HAPPEN | o result consequence. “Because / therefore / since” between clauses is LA [ cause ] consequence (the cause comes first, the word order is changed) no test.
  • A decision is the axis THINK (THINK(=+5) | i to decide, THINK(=-4) | i to doubt).

People and objects

  • Pronouns are the axis SOMEONE (I +5, you +3, he −3), plural through MANY: SOMEONE(=+5) MANY(=+3) | o is we.
  • Animacy is the axis THING (stone −5, plant 0, animal +4); speed is MOVE, excitement FEEL; whether pleasant or not is GOOD.
  • grandfather, old man = SOMEONE SEX(=+5) TIME(=-4) | o (“male ancestor”; TIME(=-4) alone gives ancestor; the earlier variant with LIVE(=+3) had four roots). No test.

Emotions, battle

  • Law and authority are RULE, battle and war are FIGHT (peace = FIGHT MANY(=-5) | o).
  • The axis of FIGHT is how serious it is: −5 game, sport, competition by the rules … 0 brawl, dispute … +5 war to the death (roundtrip_candidates, 11 → 17 exact of 24). game = FIGHT(=-5) | o, sport = FIGHT(=-5) BODY | o, player = SOMEONE FIGHT(=-5) | o, tournament = FIGHT(=-5) PART(=+5) | o, toy = THING(=-5) FIGHT(=-5) | o, champion = SOMEONE FIGHT(=-5) GOOD | o C+5; war = FIGHT(=+5) | o, weapon = FIGHT(=+5) | o tool (a tool of battle), soldier = SOMEONE FIGHT(=+5) RULE(=+4) | o, army = SOMEONE FIGHT(=+5) PART(=+5) | o.
  • Emotions: FEEL (excitement) + GOOD (pleasantness) + a root by meaning: fear TIME(+3) (something bad ahead), anger FIGHT | a, social ones SOMEONE | o (+ SEE | i); the axes NEAR and WANT do not work for direction.
  • Mood is MOOD: −5 grief, despair … −3 sadness … 0 serious … +3 merry … +5 funny (roundtrip_mood: 24 of 32 against 11 through FEEL GOOD, where the funny merged with the pleasant: pleasant, compliment, praise). funny = MOOD(=+5) | a, joke = SAY MOOD(=+5) | o, humor = MOOD(=+5) | o quality, comedy = ART MOOD(=+5) | o, laugh = MOOD(=+5) | i, cheerful = MOOD(=+3) | a, smile = MOOD(=+3) BODY | i, party = MOOD(=+3) PART(=+5) | o, celebrate = DO MOOD(=+3) PART(=+5) | i, serious = MOOD(=0) | a, sad = MOOD(=-3) | a, cry = MOOD(=-3) CONSUME(=-5) GRAIN(=-3) | i (to emit a liquid), grief = MOOD(=-5) | o, despair = MOOD(=-5) CAN(=-5) | o, mourn = MOOD(=-5) LIVE(=-5) | i, funeral = MOOD(=-5) LIVE(=-5) PART(=+5) | o, tragedy = ART MOOD(=-5) | o. Happiness is not a mood but an evaluation: happy = FEEL(=+4) GOOD(=+4) | a (MOOD(=+3) reads as cheerful). A catastrophe is an event: HAPPEN GOOD(=-5) | o. DO before MOOD in an event makes an actor (funeral with DO → mourner).

Time

  • TIME(=+1) reads as soon; do not use it for “later”.
  • later, subsequent, earlier, before, after, initially are the label R, not TIME(=±n) (roundtrip_reltime).
  • A span of time is TIME(=0) BIG(=n) | o, the BIG scale: −5 second, −4 minute, −2 hour, 0 full day, +1 week, +2 month, +3 year, +4 decade, +5 century; a moment is TIME(=0) BIG(=-5) MANY(=-1) | o (checked with MANY(=+1), “a little”, on the old scale) (roundtrip_time: 20 of 20, without the scale 7 of 20).
  • Which day exactly: the TIME level is the shift from “now”: yesterday TIME(=-1) BIG(=0) | e, tomorrow TIME(=+1) BIG(=0) | e, today TIME(=0) BIG(=0) NEAR(=+5) | e (“this day”).
  • A part of the day is a day with a BEGIN level: TIME(=0) BIG(=0) BEGIN(=n) | o: −4 morning, 0 noon, +3 evening, +5 night, midnight is BEGIN(=+5) | o C+5. Annual is TIME(=0) BIG(=+3) MANY(=+5) | a (“every year”).

Cardinal directions

“The sides of the earth”: PLACE BIG(=+5) and a direction. We stand facing the sunrise: east is the front (SIDE(=+5)), west is the back (SIDE(=-5)); north is the top of the map (ABOVE(=+5)), south the bottom (ABOVE(=-5)). East = PLACE BIG(=+5) SIDE(=+5) | o, north = PLACE BIG(=+5) ABOVE(=+5) | o, northern = PLACE BIG(=+5) ABOVE(=+5) | a. Without “earth” PLACE ABOVE(=+4) reads as top (0 of 6), with it 10 of 10 (roundtrip_time). Intermediate directions: the limit is three roots, no exception: northeast = PLACE ABOVE(=+3) SIDE(=+3) | o (no test; may read as “top-front”).

Direction of motion

With a verb of motion (MOVE, GIVE, DO), the value of INSIDE, NEAR or ABOVE is where the motion ends. To enter = MOVE INSIDE(=+5) | i, to exit = MOVE INSIDE(=-5) | i, to arrive = MOVE NEAR(=+5) | i, to leave = MOVE NEAR(=-5) | i, to go up = MOVE ABOVE(=+5) | i, to go down = MOVE ABOVE(=-5) | i; to approach = MOVE NEAR(=+5) | i A0 (has not arrived yet); to return = MOVE SAME(=+5) | i. Moving something else is the label K+3: to insert = MOVE INSIDE(=+5) | i K+3, to take out, extract = MOVE INSIDE(=-5) | i K+3, to bring = MOVE NEAR(=+5) | i K+3, to send = MOVE NEAR(=-5) | i K+3, to fill = MOVE INSIDE(=+5) MANY(=+5) | i K+3, to empty = MOVE INSIDE(=-5) MANY(=+5) | i K+3, to pour = MOVE ABOVE(=-4) GRAIN(=-3) | i K+3. A separate “from where / to where” label is not needed: with and without it 18 of 24 (roundtrip_direction).

Size, groups, parts

  • small = only BIG(=-3), without PART (roundtrip_words_isolated).
  • The axis of PART: crumb −5, part / member −2, whole / “completely” +2, group +5 (roundtrip_part_ladder, roundtrip_part_groups).
  • society = SOMEONE PART(=+5), member = SOMEONE PART(=-2), set / union = PART(=+5), herd = THING(=0) PART(=+5); not MANY SAME and not PART SOMEONE MANY. Put PART(=+5) right after the head word, not first.

Change, authority, the special

(roundtrip_new_axes, 38 of 54)

  • Change is the root CHANGE (−5 to stay … +5 to transform); the direction goes through BIG and GOOD: grow = CHANGE BIG(=+3) | i, improve = CHANGE GOOD(=+3) | i, remain = CHANGE(=-4) | i.
  • Authority and institution are the axis of RULE (−5 personal … +5 official): authority = RULE(=+4) | o, official = RULE(=+5) | a, private = RULE(=-5) | a. Weak values around 0 carry nothing.
  • Organizational types (committee, commission, agency, union, party, institution) are not distinguished by roots: the code SOMEONE PART(=+5) RULE(=+4) | o reads as “an official group”. We do not introduce an @NN vocabulary; the loss is accepted.
  • The special and the general are the axis of SAME (the root PARTICULAR was merged into it, roundtrip_merge): special / unique = SAME(=-4) | a, especially = SAME(=-4) | e I+4, individual = SAME(=-4) PART(=-2) | a, ordinary = SAME(=+3) | a, general / universal = SAME(=+4) MANY(=+5) | a. Do not assemble: specific (SAME(=-4) KNOW(=+5) → famous), rare (MANY(=+1) → few; on the new scale MANY(=-3), not checked).

Voice

  • The label V (roundtrip_voice, 10 of 11): −4 passive, 0 by itself, +4 on purpose.
  • The agent with a passive is the particle PE: … V-4 PE X (roundtrip_pe, 6 of 6, preliminary).

Degree

(roundtrip_degree, 18 of 24 correct, 2 partly)

  • An intensifier or a weakener is the label I on the head word: very good = GOOD(=+3) | a I+4, extremely big = BIG(=+3) | a I+5, slightly warm = HEAT(=+2) | a I-2, hardly possible = CAN(=+1) | a I-5, completely different = SAME(=-5) | a I+5.
  • I0 (“rather, moderately”) can get lost; for a weak degree write I+1…+2.
  • Not BIG(=+5) as “very”; not C (comparison).
  • Almost is not conveyed by the label I: it reads as quite likely / fairly certain; this is accepted as a tolerable loss, and we introduce nothing.
  • Importance is BIG + HAPPEN (“big consequences”): BIG(=+3) HAPPEN | a significant, BIG(=+4) HAPPEN | a important / major, BIG(=+5) HAPPEN | a crucial / momentous (in the test HAPPEN also had a value; in v2 it does not) (roundtrip_importance, 8–9 of 10). Not SAME(=-4) (reads as special) and not GOOD + BIG (reads as great / wonderful, 0 of 10); vital through LIVE is not read.

Substance and tools

  • The axis of GRAIN is how a substance holds together (the root MATTER was merged into it, roundtrip_merge, 17 of 24): −5 gas, steam, smoke … −3 liquid … 0 granular … +5 a solid piece. gas = GRAIN(=-5) | o, steam = GRAIN(=-5) HEAT(=+3) | o, liquid = GRAIN(=-3) | o, water = GRAIN(=-3) CONSUME(=+5) LIVE | o, sand = GRAIN(=0) THING(=-5) | o, flour = GRAIN(=0) CONSUME(=+5) THING(=0) | o, stone = GRAIN(=+5) THING(=-5) | o, ice = GRAIN(=+5) HEAT(=-5) | o, melt = CHANGE(=+4) GRAIN(=-3) HEAT(=+3) | i. Honey (GRAIN(=-3) CONSUME(=+5) GOOD(=+4)) reads as wine, juice.
  • A tool is the role tool (earlier the axis of DO, DO(=+5): 17 of 24, roundtrip_merge): the roots describe the action, | o tool makes a tool out of it. knife = JOIN(=-4) TOUCH(=+4) | o tool, saw = JOIN(=-4) MOVE(=+2) | o tool, scissors = JOIN(=-4) MANY(=-1) | o tool, hammer = TOUCH(=+5) MOVE(=+4) | o tool, brush = TOUCH(=-3) MOVE | o tool, pen = TEXT | o tool, thermometer = MEASURE HEAT | o tool, ladder = MOVE ABOVE(=+4) | o tool, key = INSIDE(=+4) CAN(=+4) | o tool. Did not assemble: spoon (→ cup), broom, needle. Not THING(=-5) for tools: it reads as stone.

Science, technology, art

(roundtrip_art, 29 of 32 correct, 1 more as a backup answer)

  • A field of knowledge or occupation is the root ART (−5 calculation, technology … +5 creativity, culture): poetry = ART(=+5) SAY | o, painting = ART(=+5) SEE GRAIN(=-3) | o, history = ART(=+4) KNOW TIME(=-3) | o, physics = ART(=-4) GRAIN MOVE | o, mathematics = ART(=-5) MANY SEE | o, engineering = ART(=-4) DO THING | o.
  • A practitioner is SOMEONE ART(=±n) …: engineer = SOMEONE ART(=-4) DO | o, scientist = SOMEONE ART(=-4) KNOW | o, artist = SOMEONE ART(=+5) DO | o.
  • Devices are THING(=-5) and an action: machine = THING(=-5) MOVE DO | o, computer = THING(=-5) THINK ART(=-5) | o.
  • Not read: architecture (ART(=0) DO PLACE → workshop; ART(=+3) PLACE LIVE → theater). Accepted as a loss.

Age, music, water, success

Blind test roundtrip_lacunae2: 23 of 24 with recipes, 11 and 13 of 24 without them.

  • Age is LIVE with TIME, where the TIME level means since when the being has been alive:
  • young: LIVE TIME(=-1) | a;
  • adult: LIVE TIME(=-3) | a;
  • old, elderly: LIVE TIME(=-5) | a;
  • youth: SOMEONE LIVE TIME(=-1) | o N+3, “young people”. The test had a variant with PART(=+5), which was read correctly (2 of 2), but that is four roots, over the limit; the three-root variant was not checked;
  • age: LIVE TIME MEASURE | o.

About things it is written without LIVE: ancient is TIME(=-5) | a. - Music is sound as an art. FEEL(=+3) separates singing from poetry (ART(=+5) SAY). If a song is written through TEXT, it reads as verse. - to sing: SAY ART(=+5) FEEL(=+3) | i, a song is the same with | o; - singer: SOMEONE SAY FEEL(=+3) | o; - music: ART(=+5) SAY THING | o; - melody: ART(=+5) SAY LONG | o; - to dance: ART(=+5) MOVE BODY | i. - Water and relief are PLACE GRAIN(=-3), “a place of liquid”. Without PLACE at the start and without a recipe such a code reads as air or sky. - sea: PLACE GRAIN(=-3) BIG(=+5) | o; - lake: PLACE GRAIN(=-3) BIG(=0) | o; - river: PLACE GRAIN(=-3) MOVE(=+3) | o; - island: PLACE GRAIN(=-3) INSIDE(=+5) | o; - shore: PLACE GRAIN(=-3) NEAR(=+4) | o; - mountain: PLACE ABOVE(=+5) BIG(=+4) | o. - Success and victory are GOOD about a result, the role result. - to win: FIGHT GOOD(=+4) | i, to lose: FIGHT GOOD(=-4) | i; - victory: FIGHT GOOD(=+4) | o result; - success: DO GOOD(=+4) | o result; - to fail: DO GOOD(=-4) | i; - famous: KNOW MANY(=+5) | a.

Law, money, food, transport

(roundtrip_lacunae3: 24 of 24 with recipes against 17 without them)

  • Law is RULE(=+4) (law) with GOOD(=-4) (violation): crime = DO GOOD(=-4) RULE(=+4) | o, criminal = SOMEONE GOOD(=-4) RULE(=+4) | o, court = PLACE RULE(=+4) THINK(=+5) | o, judge = SOMEONE RULE(=+4) THINK(=+5) | o, prison = PLACE MOVE(=-5) RULE(=+4) | o, to steal = GIVE(=-4) GOOD(=-4) | i.
  • Money is VALUE with GIVE; the head root decides what is given: money = VALUE GIVE(=0) | o, to pay = VALUE GIVE(=+4) | i (to sell is GIVE(=+3) VALUE | i), debt = VALUE GIVE(=+4) TIME(=+3) | o, rich = VALUE MANY(=+5) | a, poor = VALUE MANY(=-4) | a (a shortage of money), market = PLACE GIVE(=0) VALUE | o.
  • Food is CONSUME(=+5) with the kind of thing THING: meat = CONSUME(=+5) THING(=+4) | o, vegetable = CONSUME(=+5) THING(=0) | o, to cook = HEAT(=+3) CONSUME(=+5) | i, kitchen = PLACE HEAT(=+3) CONSUME(=+5) | o, farm = PLACE THING(=0) DO | o, to harvest = GIVE(=-4) THING(=0) PART(=+5) | i.
  • Transport and buildings are the role tool (not “stone” THING(=-5)) and PLACE: car = MOVE(=+4) | o tool, ship = MOVE GRAIN(=-3) | o tool, road = PLACE MOVE LONG(=-3) | o, bridge = PLACE MOVE ABOVE(=+4) | o, house = PLACE INSIDE(=+5) LIVE | o, room = PLACE INSIDE(=+5) PART(=-2) | o. Institutions with a recipe are written by the recipe, not in quotes.

Society, power, learning, war

(roundtrip_lacunae4: 24 of 24 with recipes against 13 and 19 without them)

  • Society: to trust = THINK(=+5) GOOD(=+4) | i (“to decide that someone is good”), friend = FRIEND | o (the test had SOMEONE WANT(=+3) JOIN(=+3) | o), ally = SOMEONE FIGHT JOIN(=+4) | o, leader = SOMEONE SIDE(=+5) RULE | o, agreement = THINK(=+5) SAME(=+5) | o, safe = GOOD(=-4) CAN(=-5) | a (“harm is impossible”).
  • Power: king = SOMEONE RULE(=+5) SEX(=+5) | o (queen is SEX(=-5)), government = SOMEONE RULE(=+5) PART(=+5) | o, to vote = THINK(=+5) MANY(=+5) | i (“everyone decides”), elections = THINK(=+5) MANY(=+5) RULE(=+4) | o, citizen = SOMEONE PART(=-2) RULE(=+4) | o, city = PLACE SOMEONE MANY(=+5) | o.
  • Learning and film: school = PLACE KNOW CONSUME(=+5) | o, student = SOMEONE KNOW CONSUME(=+5) | o, teacher = SOMEONE KNOW GIVE(=+4) | o, university = PLACE KNOW ABOVE(=+4) | o, film = ART(=+5) SEE MOVE | o, to advertise = SAY GIVE(=+3) VALUE | i (“to speak in order to sell”).
  • War: enemy = FRIEND(=-5) | o (in the test SOMEONE WANT(=-4) FIGHT | o), battle = FIGHT(=+5) PART(=-2) | o (“a part of a war”), rifle = FIGHT(=+5) HEAT(=+5) | o tool (“a fiery tool of battle”), to defend = DO GOOD(=-4) | i K-4 (“not to let harm be done”), to attack = FIGHT(=+4) BEGIN(=-5) | i.

Quotes: categories and family members; health and sleep

(roundtrip_quotes_health: 40 of 40 with the rule and recipes against 37 and 28 without them; the “quotes or roots” decisions of two coders matched in 24 of 24 against 18)

  • The quotes boundary. From roots: categories and purposes: organ = BODY INSIDE(=+5) PART(=-2) | o, game = FIGHT(=-5) | o, sport = FIGHT(=-5) BODY | o, star = HEAT(=+5) ABOVE(=+5) PART(=-2) | o, planet = PLACE ABOVE(=+5) MOVE | o, satellite = ABOVE(=+5) MOVE | o tool, company = PART(=+5) DO VALUE | o, official = SOMEONE RULE(=+4) | o, railway = PLACE MOVE LONG(=+5) | o, broadcasting = SAY GIVE(=+3) MANY(=+5) | o. In quotes: specific members of a family in the international or Latin form ("hepar" liver, "ren" kidney, "Sol", "Luna", "football", "chess", "protein") and narrow terms ("enzyme", "orbit", "Parliament"). The Moon cannot be read from roots (horizon, sunset), "Luna" gets 2 of 2.
  • Health and sleep are the middle of LIVE (“half alive”), and GOOD says which: to sleep = LIVE(=0) GOOD(=+3) | i, dream = LIVE(=0) SEE | o, to wake up = LIVE(=+5) BEGIN(=-4) | i, tired = LIVE(=0) CAN(=-3) | a, to rest = MOVE(=-5) GOOD(=+3) | i; sick = LIVE(=0) GOOD(=-4) | a, illness = LIVE(=0) GOOD(=-4) | o, patient = SOMEONE LIVE(=0) GOOD(=-4) | o, hospital = PLACE LIVE(=0) GOOD(=-4) | o, healthy = LIVE(=+5) GOOD(=+4) | a, to treat = LIVE GOOD(=+4) | i K+3, doctor = SOMEONE LIVE GOOD(=+4) | o, medicine = LIVE GOOD(=+4) | o tool, to die = LIVE(=-5) | i, wound = BODY JOIN(=-3) | o, pain = BODY FEEL(=+4) GOOD(=-4) | o. The root HEALTH is not needed.

Price, thoroughness, attachment

(roundtrip_value_care_join)

  • Price is the root VALUE (−5 cheap … +5 expensive): expensive = VALUE(=+4) | a, cheap = VALUE(=-4) | a, price = VALUE | o; 10 of 10. Buying and selling go with GIVE: buy = GIVE(=-3) VALUE | i, sell = GIVE(=+3) VALUE | i, but the decoder can confuse the direction.
  • Thoroughness is the root CARE (−5 careless … +5 thorough): carefully = CARE(=+3) | e, carelessly = CARE(=-3) | e, thoroughly = CARE(=+5) PART(=+2) | e, hastily = CARE(=-5) MOVE(=+4) | e, meticulous = CARE(=+5) PART(=-4) | a I+4; 6 of 6.
  • Attachment is the root JOIN (−5 to separate … +5 to attach): join = JOIN(=+3) | i, separate = JOIN(=-3) PART | i, remove = JOIN(=-4) | i; add / attach / unite merge, add is not read.

Attitude to people

  • The root FRIEND (−5 enemy, hostile, cruel … 0 neutral … +5 friend, friendly, kind; 2026-10, replaced TONE “rude … polite”: the centers of TONE and CARE were close, cos 0.65, for FRIEND and CARE 0.12): friend = FRIEND | o, enemy = FRIEND(=-5) | o, kind, friendly = FRIEND(=+4) | a, hostile = FRIEND(=-5) | a, cruel = FRIEND(=-5) TOUCH(=+4) | a, warm = FRIEND(=+4) HEAT(=+2) | a, cold = FRIEND(=-3) HEAT(=-3) | a.
  • Politeness is a manner of speech: polite = FRIEND(=+3) SAY CARE(=+3) | a, rude = FRIEND(=-3) SAY | a, tactful = FRIEND(=+3) CARE(=+5) | a, blunt = SAY CARE(=-3) | a.
  • Not checked: the test roundtrip_tone (26 of 32) was on the earlier TONE.

Intake and output

(roundtrip_consume, 26 of 32 correct)

  • The axis of CONSUME: −5 to emit, throw out … +5 to absorb, take in. The channel is set by the second root: eat = CONSUME(=+5) GRAIN(=+3) | i, drink = CONSUME(=+5) GRAIN(=-3) | i, inhale = CONSUME(=+5) GRAIN(=-5) | i, exhale = CONSUME(=-5) GRAIN(=-5) | i, excrete = CONSUME(=-5) BODY | i, vomit = CONSUME(=-5) BODY ABOVE(=+3) | i.
  • Speech and hearing: speak = CONSUME(=-5) SAY | i, listen = CONSUME(=+5) SAY CARE(=+3) | i.
  • Written language is the root TEXT (no axis; SAY is oral), roundtrip_candidates, 3 → 15 exact of 24: write = TEXT | i, read = CONSUME(=+5) TEXT | i, writer = SOMEONE TEXT | o, author = TEXT | o agent, book = TEXT PART(=+2) | o, letter = TEXT GIVE(=+3) SOMEONE | o, newspaper = TEXT TIME(=0) PART(=+5) | o, library = PLACE TEXT PART(=+5) | o. The earlier CONSUME(=-5) SEE (→ show) and CONSUME(=+5) SEE (→ watch) were not read.

Causation

(roundtrip_causative, 25 of 32 correct, 3 more as a backup answer)

  • The label K on a verb: the subject acts on the object E so that it performs or undergoes the action. K-5 to forbid, K-4 to prevent, K-2 to dissuade, K0 to allow, K+3 to make / persuade, K+4 to force, K+5 to compel.
  • What exactly is caused is set by the verb's root: make = DO | i K+3, prevent = DO | i K-4, feed = CONSUME | i K+4, teach = KNOW | i K+4, frighten = FEEL(=+4) GOOD(=-4) | i K+4, force = DO WANT(=-5) | i K+5, persuade = SAY THINK(=+5) | i K+3, allow = RULE(=+3) | i K0, forbid = RULE(=+4) DO | i K-5.
  • Induce / encourage (K+2…3) get confused with have / get / entice.

Concrete and abstract

There is no root ABSTRACT (v2; earlier the axis −5 tangible … +5 abstract, roundtrip_abstract, 25 of 32). No test: idea = THINK | o result, principle = RULE THINK | o, imply = SAY THINK | i, tangible = TOUCH CAN(=+4) | a, abstract = TOUCH CAN(=-5) | a, theoretical = KNOW THINK | a, shake = MOVE(=+2) BODY | i, kick = TOUCH(=+4) MOVE(=+4) BODY | i; assume / suppose = THINK(=+3) | i M-1.

Concept recipes (two blind tests: roundtrip_and_mod, round 2)

  • Work: game, now FIGHT(=-5) | o (FIGHT has got an axis, see “Emotions, battle”; the earlier FIGHT ART(=+2) | o was also read); independent: SOMEONE(=+5) CAN(=+5) JOIN(=-5) | a (2/2); help: DO GOOD(=+3) | i; exception: SAME(=-4) JOIN(=-5) RULE | o (2/2 in roundtrip_merge); equality: SAME(=+5) | o quality; economic: VALUE GIVE(=0) | a (1/2).
  • Measures and ratios are the root MEASURE (axis: −5 a quantity in itself … +5 a quantity relative to another; introduced after two failures with PART/BIG/SAME; blind test v2 26 of 32, roundtrip_measure): ratio MEASURE(=+5) | o, percentage MEASURE(=+5) PART(=-2) | o, rate MEASURE(=+5) TIME | o, amount MEASURE(=-5) | o, size MEASURE(=-5) BIG | o, degree, level MEASURE(=0) | o, average MEASURE(=+5) MANY SAME | o, measurement and to measure MEASURE DO | o / MEASURE | i, unit MEASURE SAME | o. Weak: meter, dimension, proportional (read as distance/area/fractional).
  • Do not work (two series of recipes each, none read correctly): way/method, measurement as an act of measuring (now MEASURE(=-5), but it reads as meter / unit), a sort of X (I-2 on a noun does not read as “a kind”; a sort of bird → bird). RULE(=-5)/SAME(=-5) RULE for “independent” reads as illegal.
